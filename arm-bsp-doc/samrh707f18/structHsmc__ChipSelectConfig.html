uint16_t totalWriteCycle
 It defines the duration of write cycle as (256 * bit[8:7] + bit[6:0]) clock cycles. Additionally it implicitly defines the duration of hold step of both NWE and NCS signals in Write access as a difference between the duration of write cycle and sum of durations of setup and pulse steps of NWE and NCS signals respectively. Due to duration of write cycle being equal to sum of setup, pulse and hold steps durations of NWE and NCS signals, it must not be less than sum of setup and pulse steps durations of both NWE and NCS signals. Value must be in range 1-511.
 
uint16_t totalReadCycle
 It defines the duration of read cycle as (256 * bit[8:7] + bit[6:0]) clock cycles. Additionally it implicitly defines the duration of hold step of both NRD and NCS signals in Read access as a difference between the duration of read cycle and sum of durations of setup and pulse steps of NRD and NCS signals respectively. Due to duration of read cycle being equal to sum of setup, pulse and hold steps durations of NRD and NCS signals, it must not be less than sum of setup and pulse steps durations of both NRD and NCS signals. Value must be in range 1-511.
